Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · עברית

In short: AgeNow does not collect, store or send any personal information to external servers. All your data stays on your device only. The only thing collected is anonymous visit statistics, without cookies.

1. Information we collect

AgeNow does not collect any personal information. The data you enter (names and dates of birth) is stored locally only on your device, using your browser's LocalStorage, and is never sent to any server.

2. How the information is used

The information you enter is used only to calculate the age shown on the screen. It is not used for anything else.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer any information to third parties. The app does not include:

  • Ads
  • Third-party SDKs
  • An internet connection to make the app work

Visit statistics: the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits anonymously (for example the number of visits, device type, country and page load speed). It does not use cookies, does not identify users or track them across sites, and does not receive the names or dates of birth you enter. Learn more in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

4. Access to your device

The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, contacts or any other permission. The only thing it does is read from and write to your browser's LocalStorage.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Tapping "Remove" for each person in the app
  • Clearing your browser or app data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect information about children or adults.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
