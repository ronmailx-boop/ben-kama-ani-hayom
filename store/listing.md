## English (en-US)

**App name** (30 max)

```
AgeNow: Exact Age Calculator
```

**Short description** (80 max)

```
See anyone's exact age in years, months and days. Private and works offline.
```

**Full description** (4000 max)

```
AgeNow shows the exact age of the people you care about, in years, months and days, always up to date.

Add your kids, your parents, your partner or your friends with just a name and a date of birth. Each person gets a tab, and one tap shows their exact age.

FEATURES
• Exact age in years, months and days
• Up to 5 people, each in their own tab
• Updates by itself every day, nothing to recalculate
• Clean dark design that is easy to read
• Works offline
• English and Hebrew

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up and no account
• Names and dates of birth are stored only on your phone
• Nothing you enter is ever sent to a server
• No ads

Handy for kids' ages in months, filling in forms, or just answering "how old is she now, exactly?"
```
